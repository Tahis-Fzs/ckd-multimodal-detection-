FR-06
CKD Risk
Prediction
The system shall use the selected primary model
to output CKD-related risk estimates for the
supported clinical and hospital branches as the
core decision-support function of the framework.
High
FR-07
Probability
Calibration
The system shall apply post-hoc probability
calibration, especially for the primary MIMIC-IV
model, so that predicted risk values are suitable
for interpretation as probabilities rather than
uncalibrated classification scores.
High
FR-08
Multimodal Late
Fusion
The system shall combine branch-level prediction
probabilities through a late-fusion protocol when
multimodal integration is evaluated, because the
public cohorts are disjoint and same-patient raw-
feature fusion is not available.
Medium
FR-09
Explainability
Generation
The system shall generate prediction explanations
and feature-importance summaries, including
global and local attributions for the primary
hospital model, to support transparent research-
oriented clinical decision support.
High
FR-10
Evaluation and
Reporting
The system shall evaluate models using
discrimination, classification, and calibration
metrics, including AUROC, AUPRC, F1-score,
sensitivity, specificity, Brier score, and expected
calibration error, to enable fair comparison
beyond accuracy alone.
High
FR-11
Prototype
Visualization
The system shall provide an interactive research
prototype that presents calibrated risk output
together with explanation views for stakeholder
demonstration in a decision-support context; the
implemented interface supports the primary
MIMIC branch.
High
