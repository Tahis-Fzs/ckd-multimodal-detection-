Organization of the Report
There are six chapters in this report.
Chapter 1 provides an introduction to CKD, reasons for developing a method for early risk prediction,
research goals, methodology, primary outcomes, and the structure of the report.
Existing research works related to CKD prediction, deep learning in healthcare, the EHR/wearable
system, multimodal fusion techniques, clinical decision-support systems and explainable AI are
reviewed in chapter 2 and a gap analysis is presented for the proposed framework.
The research methodology, datasets (NHANES, MIMIC-IV, WESAD), preprocessing, feature
engineering, model development, Late-Fusion protocol, explainability using SHAP values, and
evaluation design, and the project plan are discussed in Chapter 3.
Chapter 4 contains the implementation details, experimental setup, results for each branch,
explainability and calibration analysis and the Streamlit decision-support prototype.
Chapter 5 covers engineering standards, ethical and societal issues, project management and
engineering of projects with complex mapping.
Results are summarized and limitation is given in chapter 6, which also includes the directions for
work to be continued which includes the clinical validation and aligned Multimodal data collection.
