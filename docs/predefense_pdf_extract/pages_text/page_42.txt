4.3.4
Robustness and Temporal Validation
The results of the supplementary validations are given in Table 4.5. There were no inconsistencies in
discriminating the resampled groups of subjects for the logistic regression classifier with AUROC of
0.769 ± 0.007 (95% CI: [0.764, 0.774]) and an F1 score of 0.401 ± 0.015 from grouped-split
experiments with five different seeds. The results from the temporal external validation including
6126 admissions of subjects who first came in contact with the hospital after the 80th percentile
hospital-to-hospital admission time, were AUROC 0.771, F1 0.442, recall 0.715, specificity 0.680,
and ECE 0.022, with an improvement of +0.010 AUROC compared to AUROC in the grouped-split
test AUROC 0.761.
These results show that there was not significant loss of the discrimination at the level of admission
under either random partitions of the subjects or forward-in-time holds out, which involved subjects in
the same hospital database. They do not, however, stand in for the external institutional validations
which happen in another institution [6].
