Summary
From literature scanned it has been predicted that prediction of CKD is shifted from conventional ML
which only works on structured clinical data, use of wearables has been beneficial in terms of
continuous monitoring of physiological parameters using deep learning with the use of huge datasets
of EHR. There's no design, though, and things such as integrating EHR with wearables, adding a
CKD label to wearables, explainability, and readiness for Clinical deployment, are not being
addressed. These gaps inspire us to design the envisioned explainable multi-modal approach for CKD
risk prediction with features derived from clinical data, hospital EHRs and features derived from
wearables and lastly presenting the output and explanation using SHAP.
