FR ID

FR-01
Multimodal Data
Ingestion
The system shall ingest population clinical data
from NHANES, hospital electronic health record
data from MIMIC-IV, and wearable-related data
from WESAD to support the three modality
branches of the framework.
High
FR-02
Data
Preprocessing and
Splitting
For each dataset, the system shall apply branch-
specific preprocessing, including missing-value
handling, leakage elimination, subject-level
grouped train–validation–test splitting, and
feature normalization or standardization before
model training.
High
FR-03
CKD Label
Construction
The system shall construct CKD-related binary
labels for the clinical branches using clinically
accepted criteria, including eGFR-based
population labelling and admission-level hospital
proxy definitions consistent with the project
methodology.
High
FR-04
Modality Feature
Extraction
The system shall convert clinical, EHR, and
wearable inputs into structured feature
representations, including demographic and
laboratory attributes, admission-level
observations, and window-based physiological
statistics, through separate feature pipelines for
each modality.
High
FR-05
Model Training
and Comparison
The system shall train and compare branch-
appropriate models, including calibrated logistic
regression, random forest, XGBoost, tabular
MLP, and residual MLP for clinical and hospital
branches, and random forest for the wearable
branch, to select a primary model per branch
under the available data constraints.
High
