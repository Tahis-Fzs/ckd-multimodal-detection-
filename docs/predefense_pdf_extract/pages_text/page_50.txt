K1 Natural science: This included the application of CKD phenomena and renal physiology to
feature labelling and interpretation and the writing of limitations for the labels [2], [17], [18].

K2 Mathematics: These models were all built with logistic regression, sigmoid calibration,
tabular deep models with crossentropy training along with knowledge of statistics and
optimization techniques such as AUROC,AUPRC, ECE and Brier [14], [15].

K3 Engineering fundamentals: The pipeline has been preprocessed with the branches and has
been trained only the grouped validation, and the explicit failure handling for the missing
laboratory values have been applied too.

K4 Specialist knowledge: The knowledge of the admissions recorded in MIMIC hospital
admission tables, the NHANES overlays on the table, the modality of the WESAD wrist window
and the leakage grouping of the subject or participants played an important role.

K5 Engineering design: The original contribution consists of 100% new multimodal CKD-risk
framework, completely new branch pipelines, complete late fusion from disjoint cohorts and
calibrated primary modelling and decisions supporting interface.

K6 Engineering practice: Some of the implementation methods used were: experimental staged
process, frozen checkpoints, exported CSV and JSON outputs, and locally prototype generation
and testing.

K8 Research literature: The literature that inspired the development of gap analysis and
methodological decision was from fields of CKD prediction, machine learning based on EHR,
Explainable Clinical AI, multimodal fusion, and etc. [1], [3], [39], [49].
